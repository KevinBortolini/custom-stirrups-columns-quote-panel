Version 1

I built a budgeting workflow web application for custom stirrups and columns, designed to reduce the time required to calculate, register, and formalize quotes for highly variable fabricated items.

In this type of operation, each stirrup and each column can vary by format, dimensions, steel gauge, spacing, quantity of bars, quantity of pieces, and customer type. Without software support, that turns quote preparation into a slow and repetitive manual process.

This project centralizes the operational flow in a single dashboard: client data, custom item entry, quote calculation, saved quote history, reopening for review, safe duplication of previous quotes, and browser-based PDF generation.

From an engineering perspective, I focused on preserving calculation behavior while improving the usability of the interface, organizing the workflow around a dashboard layout, and adding searchable quote history backed by SQLite.

Stack: FastAPI, SQLite3, Vite, vanilla JavaScript.

This public repository is a sanitized portfolio version of an internal-style application, with confidential business information replaced by fictional demo data.

New portfolio project:
Budget Panel for Custom Stirrups and Columns

This is a sanitized public version of an internal-style budgeting application I worked on for a quote workflow involving custom stirrups and columns.

The product solves a practical operational problem: when each item has its own dimensions, gauges, spacing, quantities, and customer conditions, generating a formal quote quickly becomes difficult without dedicated software.

The application supports:
- quote assembly through a dashboard workflow
- calculation of custom stirrup and column items
- quote persistence in SQLite
- history lookup and reopening
- safe duplication of historical quotes into new quotes
- admin-only pricing controls
- browser-based PDF generation

Stack: FastAPI, SQLite3, Vite, vanilla JavaScript.
